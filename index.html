Mission Brief

America is a broken conveyor belt of surveillance towers, battery farms, and vending machines that somehow survived better than Congress.

You lead the last westbound convoy. If you can cross the ruins and reach the California Core, you may be able to shut the machine mind down before humanity becomes a trivia category.

  • Each turn costs time, food, and water.
  • Travel pushes you west but chews up the rig.
  • Scavenge, repair, and rest when the road gets ugly.
  • Events offer choices with upside, downside, and occasional emotional damage.
